DHNT Inc. builds dhnt: a language and system for turning the computers you
already own into one coherent, self-hostable, agent-operable mesh. Cloud
services coordinate access; your apps, models, and data stay on
infrastructure you control. Its foundation is bashy, the open-source agentic
shell ([github.com/dhnt](https://github.com/dhnt)).

## Founder & team {#team}

### Qiang Li, Founder & CEO

Qiang founded DHNT Inc. in 2026 and leads product and engineering. He has 27
years of experience shipping production software: Oracle; IBM, as architect
and tech lead for Lowe's, Costco, GM and Sony; Cyanogen; GE Aviation, as
Predix staff engineer and architect; and 10x Genomics, where he built the
Cloud Analysis backend, UI and CLI. He was the technical cofounder of two
earlier startups and holds an M.Sc. from the National University of
Singapore. He is based in the San Francisco Bay Area.
